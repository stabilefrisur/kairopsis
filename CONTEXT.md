# Kairopsis

Kairopsis supports relative-value investigation and the development of investment views for team discussion.

## Language

**Relative Value Relationship**:
An economically meaningful comparison between sectors, segments, or exposures whose pricing is examined for relative attractiveness, including credit exposures relative to rates and FX.

**Relationship Family**:
A set of Relative Value Relationships that share an explicit economic rationale for comparison.
Comparisons between credit exposures in different currencies remain credit-credit relationships.

**Cross-Market Inconsistency**:
A difference in the economic implications of pricing across markets under a stated interpretation, even when no clear trade expression has been identified.

**Comparison Basis**:
The currency and reference-curve conventions underlying a metric, including any rebasing applied to make it comparable across markets.

**Flag**:
An observed condition or change in a single asset or a Relative Value Relationship that merits investigation, with an identifiable reason for drawing attention to it. Flags include extreme historical percentiles and significant changes in asset measures or pairwise ratios, differences, and regression residuals.
_Avoid_: Investment recommendation

**Economic Materiality**:
The significance of an observed level or change to an investment argument, judged using measures appropriate to its Relationship Family.

**Catch-up**:
A retrospective examination of the most significant market and relationship changes over a user-selected period.
_Avoid_: Unread alert backlog

**Relative Value View**:
An investment judgment about the attractiveness of one sector, segment, or exposure relative to another, supported by evidence and an economic argument.
_Avoid_: Signal when an investment judgment is meant

**Possible Expression**:
A proposed way to express a Relative Value View through market exposures, without account-specific sizing or suitability analysis.
_Avoid_: Account Expression

**Idea**:
A saved investigation that develops and revisits a Relative Value View, collecting supporting and challenging evidence, arguments, and possible expressions.
_Avoid_: Individual flag or chart when referring to the whole investigation

**Research Asset**:
A chart, snapshot, note, argument, or narrative retained as part of an Idea.

**Snapshot**:
A frozen record of chart evidence at a particular time, including the underlying observations, analytical choices, and Comparison Basis.
_Avoid_: Live chart

**Research Pack**:
A selection of an Idea's evidence and arguments assembled to support narrative drafting outside Kairopsis.
_Avoid_: Standardized report for team distribution

**Discussion Shortlist**:
The Ideas selected for upcoming team conversations or meetings, independently of their investigation state.

**Revisit Condition**:
A stated condition whose fulfillment warrants renewed attention to an Idea.


**Data Series**:
A time-ordered set of observations for a particular asset measure, with an identified source, units and Comparison Basis.

**Analysis**:
A defined investigation of one or two Data Series, including its calculation, analytical settings and Economic Rationale where recorded. Its type is Standalone or Pair.

**Economic Rationale**:
The investment question an Analysis investigates, the reasoning for examining its Data Series, and the assumptions or alternative explanations that qualify its interpretation. It is a hypothesis to assess against evidence.

**Screening Run**:
A dated review of a defined set of Analyses, retaining which were monitored, their evaluated evidence and any limitations or failures. It preserves the context for reviewing that screening later.

**Screening Brief**:
A concise interpretation of a Screening Run, grouping significant developments, examining supporting and challenging evidence, and identifying follow-up investigations. Its conclusions are qualified by the run's coverage and data quality.

**Risk Adjustment**:
Scaling of input levels, changes or percentage changes using a risk measure or sensitivity estimated from explicitly selected absolute or percentage changes, to support comparison between exposures.

**Frequency**:
The daily, weekly or monthly interval over which an Analysis measures changes or returns. Risk Adjustment estimates use the same interval.
_Avoid_: Measurement horizon

**Standalone**:
An Analysis of one Data Series, examining its level or changes relative to its history.

**Pair**:
An Analysis relating two specified Data Series through a ratio, difference or regression.
